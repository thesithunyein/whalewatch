## 🎬 RECORDING TIPS

- **Cursor highlight:** Windows Magnifier (Win++) or use OBS cursor effect
- **Smooth transitions:** Pre-stage browser tabs (Solscan tab loaded ahead of time)
- **No filler words:** Edit out "um/uh/like" in DaVinci Resolve (free) or Clipchamp
- **Music:** Optional — chill lo-fi at 20% volume from YouTube Audio Library
- **End frame:** Hold on the live URL for 3 seconds at the end
